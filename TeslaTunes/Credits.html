TeslaTunes is a utility for quickly and easily
copying for use in your Model S all compatible
files from your iTunes music library.
It will additionally convert any Apple Lossless files
it encounters into Model S compatible Flac files.

Audio libraries: FLAC 1.5.0 and TagLib 1.13.1.
License notices are included in ThirdPartyNotices.txt in the app resources.
